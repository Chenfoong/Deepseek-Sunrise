Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Cambridge IGCSE? ▼
The Cambridge IGCSE is the world's most popular international qualification for 14–16 year olds. It is recognized by leading universities and employers worldwide.
What ages do you accept? ▼
We welcome students from ages 7 to 18, covering primary through secondary education with the Cambridge pathway.
How small are the classes? ▼
Our classes maintain a maximum 1:8 teacher-to-student ratio, ensuring every child receives personalized attention.
Do you offer trial classes? ▼
Yes! We encourage families to book a trial class so your child can experience our warm learning environment firsthand.
